# RISU Verify roadmap after infrastructure v0.8

This roadmap separates **scientific falsification**, **productization**, and **external adoption** so that product pressure cannot silently weaken the experimental protocol.

## Gate A — Infrastructure v0.8 qualification

Complete only when PR-head and merged-main CI both pass the v0.8 qualification workflow.

Deliverables:

- mechanically generated primary hashes;
- provenance reachability preflight;
- explicit-bound-evidence-only compilation;
- deterministic full compiled-case archive;
- one-command non-verdict seal;
- manual-only sealed primary dispatch.

## Gate B — Unit 002-M paired mutation control

Purpose: attack detector sensitivity **and specificity** before hunting a new real result.

Predeclare a mutation matrix containing two classes:

1. **semantic-loss positive controls** — remove or misplace a consequential distinction and require RISU to produce regression/incomplete assurance with the expected witness class;
2. **non-semantic negative controls** — schema-compatible presentation, naming, ordering, or refactor changes that preserve the declared consequence and must not be called regressions.

Primary metrics:

- true-positive rate over predeclared semantic-loss mutations;
- false-positive rate over predeclared non-semantic mutations;
- witness localization accuracy;
- deterministic repeatability;
- verifier + harness runtime.

No mutation may be selected after seeing detector output.

## Gate C — Unit 002-R real prospective falsification

Select a new real target independently of Unit 002-M outcomes. Freeze source semantics, target evidence, bounded worlds, effect cut, and claim boundary before observing the RISU verdict.

The objective is not to obtain a regression. `PRESERVED`, `CONSEQUENCE_REGRESSION`, and `INCOMPLETE_ASSURANCE` are equally admissible results.

## Gate D — `risu diff` + witness UX

Promote semantic comparison to a first-class product surface only after Unit 002-M establishes detector behavior.

`risu diff` should answer in one command:

- what consequential distinction changed;
- which admitted world first witnesses the change;
- whether the change is preserved, contradicted, or under-evidenced;
- what proof/certificate identities support that answer.

Human UX target: a competent maintainer should understand the minimal witness in under 30 seconds without reading the formal machinery.

## Gate E — repair synthesis + reverify

Generate implementation-direction suggestions from certified repair obligations while keeping the epistemic boundary explicit:

- certified necessary condition;
- suggested implementation direction;
- not-certified implementation choice.

Any proposed repair must be reverified; a suggestion is never promoted to a claim merely because it was generated by RISU.

## Gate F — cross-carrier real case

Run a prospective case outside the first carrier family to demonstrate that the semantic substrate is not MCP-specific.

Carrier selection should maximize consequence-layer comparability while differing in transport/API conventions.

## Gate G — first external maintainer loop

Target the smallest credible closed loop:

`real issue or change → RISU witness → maintainer-understandable report → fix or accepted interpretation → PR/CI integration`

The adoption claim is not “someone viewed the report.” The strongest first signal is an external maintainer changing or validating consequential behavior and accepting RISU into a real development workflow.

## Ordering rule

Do not move product polish ahead of scientific calibration when the polish depends on unvalidated detector behavior. Do not delay a clear external maintainer opportunity merely to increase corpus count if the protocol can already support the case prospectively.
